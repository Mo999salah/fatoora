# Fatoora - Product Brief

## Codename

Fatoora

> The final product name may change later. For now, Fatoora is used as a working codename.

## One-line Description

A mobile-first invoicing app that helps Arab freelancers create professional Arabic and English invoices and share them with clients through WhatsApp or email.

## Target Users

Arab freelancers and small service providers, such as:

- Frontend developers
- Designers
- Content writers
- Translators
- Digital marketers
- Photographers
- Consultants

## Problem

Many Arab freelancers still create invoices manually using Word, Excel, copied templates, or informal WhatsApp messages.

Existing invoicing tools are often:

- Too complex
- Web-first instead of mobile-first
- Not designed for Arabic UX
- Weak in RTL support
- Too focused on full accounting instead of quick invoicing
- Paid or overpowered for small freelancers

## Product Promise

Create a professional Arabic or English invoice in under one minute and share it directly with a client.

## Core Value

Fatoora helps freelancers look professional, save time, and avoid manual invoice formatting.

## MVP Focus

The first version should focus only on:

- Authentication
- Business profile setup
- Client management
- Invoice creation
- Invoice item calculation
- PDF generation
- WhatsApp/email sharing
- Basic invoice status tracking

## Non-goals for MVP

The first version will not include:

- Online payments
- Subscriptions
- Team accounts
- Inventory
- Advanced accounting
- ZATCA compliance
- Tax authority integrations
- Web dashboard
- AI invoice generation

## Success Criteria

The MVP is successful if a user can:

1. Create an account
2. Add their business information
3. Add a client
4. Create an invoice with multiple items
5. Generate a clean Arabic or English PDF
6. Share the invoice through WhatsApp or email
7. Mark the invoice as paid, unpaid, draft, or overdue
